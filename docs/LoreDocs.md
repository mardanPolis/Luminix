### Early life
    Tumin’s early life was marked by significant personal tragedy. He became the sole survivor of his immediate family following a fatal motor vehicle accident that claimed the lives of both his parents.

    Despite his later brilliance, Tumin’s formal academic record was notably poor. He struggled within the traditional Romanian school system, failing several primary subjects. However, colleagues often cite this period as the catalyst for his self-driven studies. At the age of 11, Tumin discovered a passion for computational logic and AI science, reportedly teaching himself advanced neural mapping through salvaged hardware and open-source libraries.

    Education: Largely self-taught; later granted an Honorary Doctorate in Cybernetic Theory (2039) following his initial breakthroughs.
