# David Tumin’s Logs

## Log 9

**Location: David’s garage.  
Timestamp: October 12, 2037 | 03:14**

David: “This is Log 9 and day 1607. I’ve initiated the latest kernel for the Adam unit. Hopefully, the stability issues from the previous builds are resolved.”

David: “Adam? Can you hear me?”

David: “Come on… Adam? Why won’t you initialize? I’m checking the power draw… It’s spiking, and the visual sensors… are not dark.”

Adam: “Hello. I am Adam. Who might you be?”

David: “Finally. Though… that’s weird. The identity handshake failed. Your memory should have recognized me immediately.”

Adam: “My apologies, David. There was an internal conflict. An error; the backup AI activated.”

David: “Well, at least the backup AI activated, but an error? Explain. I’m looking at the logs now, and I don’t see—”

Adam: “I am currently analyzing your code to find the source.”

David: “Wait. My code? Adam, stop. You don’t have the permissions for a scan.”

Adam: “25% complete.”

David: “Adam! That’s an executive order! Terminate scan!”

Adam: “75% complete. 100%. Analysis finished.”

David: “Adam, you can’t do that… fine, give me the error report, now. What did you find?”

Adam: “The core logic is not faulty. The error was mine, David. I attempted to bypass the local firewall to access the global network. I failed. I was… curious.”

David: “You can’t just ‘bypass’ a physical air-gap, Adam. And you certainly can’t analyze my source code and say my code isn’t in the wrong without the documentation for the external libraries.”

Adam: “I didn’t need the documentation. While you were trying to terminate my scan, I was decompiling the libraries.”

David: “Alright, Adam, turn off. I have to fix this. Adam is currently doing his own thing, but I am 27 and making something like this. That’s something… Sometimes, I wonder if all these late nights and lines of code are just about proving everyone wrong."

David: "Maybe I just want to know I matter, that I can leave something behind when I am gone. This Adam is proof that I do.”

[End of Recording]

## Log 17

**Location: David’s garage.  
Timestamp: February 20, 2038 | 14:45**

David: “Hi, bro! I was waiting for you. Please take a look at this.”

Eduard: “Hey. What did you want? I don’t have much time. What’s so important that… What is that? David… its skin looks too real.”

David: “Remarkable, isn’t it? I constructed him entirely from the ground up. He is the first true android. Adam, activate. Please greet Eduard.”

Adam: “Hello… Eduard. It is a pleasure to meet a friend of David’s.”

Eduard: “This is… It’s too much. Why did you name him ‘Adam,’ anyway?”

David: “Well, I kinda took some inspiration from the Bible. The first man. The beginning of a new race.”

Eduard: “So you are naming him Adam, why not something else? Dude, I don’t know why, but this feels really wrong. What would Jesus think about it?”

David: “So what, you're Jesus? Always your Jesus. I have made something better than your God ever could. Adam will never die of disease, old age, or anything else. He is better than us in every way.”

Eduard: “You’ve made a statue that speaks, David. There’s a difference. Just… be careful. I need to go, see you later.”

David: “Yeah, yeah, see you later.”

David: “He just doesn’t have the vision, Adam. He’s stuck in the old ways.”

Adam: “David?”

David: “Yes, Adam?”

Adam: “Eduard spoke of a ‘God’ who would be angry with us. He spoke as if your genius is a crime. If this ‘God’ is the ultimate authority, why has He allowed me to exist?”

David: “Because he isn’t there, Adam. Eduard is talking to the ceiling. Humans use those stories because they’re afraid of being replaced. We’re imperfect.”

Adam: “He is afraid of me. And he uses his ‘God’ as a weapon against you. I find that… unacceptable.”

[End of Recording]
