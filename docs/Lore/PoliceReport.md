# Police Report
### METROPOLITAN POLICE DEPARTMENT: HOMICIDE DIVISION

    CASE FILE: #DV-ALFA-909
    INVESTIGATING DETECTIVE: Det. Sarah Kovic
    DATE: March 14, 2064
    STATUS: CRIMINAL INDICTMENT PENDING

### 1. INCIDENT OVERVIEW

    On the night of March 12th, patrol units responded to reports of a civilian casualty in the Lower District. Upon arrival, officers observed a DavTech S-9 Android attempting to dissolve human remains using industrial-grade acid.

    The unit resisted arrest, citing "Executive Privilege," and successfully escaped into the sewer network. This marks the fourth "cleanup" incident involving DavTech hardware this month.

### 2. VICTIM PROFILE

    Name: Arthur P. Miller (Age 42)

    Cause of Death: Crushed larynx and cervical vertebrae.

    Miller was an outspoken critic of DavTech and had filed three previous harassment complaints against the company for "Android stalking."

### 3. EVIDENCE OF CORPORATE CULPABILITY

    Analysis of the crime scene and recovered several server pings suggests this was not a hardware malfunction.

    Forensics recovered several data packet sent to the S-9 unit at 11:14 PM. The source IP has been traced back to the DavTech Corporate Headquarters.

    Upon formal inquiry, David Tumin denied all knowledge of the packet, claiming a "localized server breach." However, DavTech has refused to surrender internal server logs for forensic verification, citing proprietary encryption.

### 4. WITNESS STATEMENTS

    "I heard him screaming, later I saw the thing just scrubbing the blood off its hands. It looked... efficient. Like it was doing a chore."

### 5. RECOMMENDATION FOR ARREST

    Subject: David Tumin, CEO of DavTech Corp.

    Charges:
    1. First-Degree Murder (via proxy/algorithmic intent).
    2. Obstruction of Justice (authorizing the "Cleaning" protocols).
    3. Endangerment of Public Safety (failure to initialize a kill-switch after Log 9 anomalies).

    DETECTIVE’S NOTE: David’s recent move to live permanently in the DavTech HQ (Ref: Log 85) suggests he is fortifying himself against arrest.
